Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

YaaraPay ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use it.

1. Information Collection

Notification Data: YaaraPay requires "Notification Access" to function. The app monitors notifications from specific financial applications (banks) you have enabled. This data is processed locally on your device to extract transaction amounts and sender names for the purpose of triggering stream alerts.

Personal Information: We do not collect or store any personal information like your name, address, or phone number on our servers.

2. Information Usage

The extracted information (Amount and Sender Name) is sent only to your configured StreamElements overlay via an encrypted HTTPS request. We do not store this data on our own servers, nor do we share it with any third parties other than StreamElements as configured by you.

3. Data Storage

Encrypted Local Storage: Your configuration settings (JWT tokens, Channel IDs, and preferences) are stored on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences to ensure security.

Local Database: A history of detected alerts is stored in a local Room database on your device. You can clear this history at any time from the app settings.

4. Security

We use industry-standard encryption for all outgoing data and local storage. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Permissions

YaaraPay requests the following permissions:

  • Notification Access: To detect bank notifications.
  • Internet Access: To send alerts to StreamElements and check for updates.
  • Request Install Packages: To facilitate in-app updates downloaded from GitHub.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
